About Seven Stars Logistics

Established in 2009, M/s Seven Stars Logistics Pvt Ltd is an Indian freight forwarding and logistics enterprise dedicated to precision, regulatory excellence, and single-window import-export execution.

Our Heritage

Built on Perfection, Trust, and Sailing Reliability

From a humble beginning in 2009, Seven Stars Logistics Pvt Ltd has continuously set higher benchmarks in the freight and logistics industry. Our founding mandate was clear: provide a true 'complete freight solution' that empowers exporters and importers to meet critical sailing deadlines and flight schedules with zero uncertainty.

We go the extra mile to maintain the delivery schedule as per each client’s instruction. Whether dealing with routine containerized shipments or complex time-sensitive Over-Dimensional and Dangerous Goods, we ensure your cargo is managed with meticulous professionalism and cost efficiency.

Strategic Proximity

Direct Port & Customs Gate Presence

Location is everything in freight forwarding. Our operational headquarters is stationed at 2nd Floor, Door No:2, Krishnan Koil St, George Town, Chennai — directly adjacent to the Chennai Customs House and facing the entrance of the Chennai Sea Port.

This geographical positioning means zero transit downtime. While off-site agents lose critical hours shuttling back and forth across city traffic, our licensed specialists attend physical examinations, address queries with customs appraisers, and oversee container release in real time.

Our People

Specialized Customer Support & Field Operations

Qualified under rigorous industry regulations to navigate complex freight movements with consummate ease.

Customer Support Team

Our dedicated Customer Support team holds in-depth mastery in Air Freight, Sea Freight, Special Economic Zone (SEZ) Cargo, Over-Dimensional Cargo, Special Equipment, and Dangerous Goods.

Formally qualified in IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR) and Customs House Agents Regulations, our staff ensures whether you are new to importing and exporting or managing high-frequency complex freight movements, your requirements are handled cost-effectively and professionally.

Operations Team

Our ground operations personnel possess extensive experience handling both export and import shipments across Seaports, International Airports, Container Freight Stations (CFS), Inland Container Depots (ICD), and Special Economic Zones (SEZ).

They maintain hands-on coordination with terminal operators, vessel handlers, shipping lines, and airline ground agents to safeguard container turnaround, seal security, and bill of lading releases.
