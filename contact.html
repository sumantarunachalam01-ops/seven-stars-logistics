Arunachalam RG

Director

M/s SEVEN STARS LOGISTICS PVT LTD • MBA in International Business • Customs Law Specialist

Arunachalam RG is the Director of Seven Stars Logistics Pvt. Ltd., holding an MBA in International Business and standing as a qualified professional in Customs Law.

With extensive knowledge and experience in International Freight Forwarding and Logistics, he has specialized expertise across Air Freight, Sea Freight, Customs Clearance, Special Economic Zone (SEZ) Cargo, Over-Dimensional Cargo (ODC), Special Equipment, and Dangerous Goods (IATA DGR).

His strong leadership, profound industry knowledge, and commitment to customer service have played a key role in the growth and success of Seven Stars Logistics.
