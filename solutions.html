Tailored Freight Solutions for Specialized Cargo

From aircraft components and hazardous chemicals to Special Economic Zone movements, Seven Stars Logistics handles complex cargo requiring verified regulatory expertise.

Sector Competence

Specialized Industry Domains We Support

Grounded strictly in verified cargo types handled by our licensed operations team.

Aircraft Parts, Spares & AOG Logistics

Airlines and MRO facilities cannot afford grounded aircraft. Seven Stars specializes in urgent Aircraft On Ground (AOG) forwarding: rotable components, avionics, landing gear spares, and engine assemblies cleared through airside customs round-the-clock.

  • • Priority 24/7 airside clearance at Indian international airports
  • • Strict adherence to airline chain of custody and traceability documents
  • • Immediate hand-carry / express tarmac transit

Special Economic Zones (SEZ) & EOU Logistics

Navigating SEZ customs requires thorough understanding of the SEZ Act 2005 and Online SEZ portals. Our team handles procurement from DTA (Domestic Tariff Area), bonded transit to SEZ developer zones, and zero-duty import clearances.

  • • SEZ online bill of entry & DTA sale documentation
  • • Authorized escort and bonded container transit to SEZ gates
  • • Duty drawback and zero-rating compliance assistance

Dangerous Goods & Hazmat Handling (IATA DGR / IMDG)

Our customer support and operations staff hold formal qualifications in IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR) and maritime IMDG standards. We oversee UN packaging specifications, MSDS reviews, and airline approvals.

  • • Certified Shipper's Declaration for Dangerous Goods (DGD)
  • • Flammable, corrosive, toxic, and lithium battery compliance
  • • Port hazardous yard storage coordination and carrier approvals

Automotive Logistics & Vehicle Imports

Chennai is the automotive hub of South Asia. Seven Stars supports automakers, tier-1 suppliers, and specialty importers with vehicle shipping, Ro-Ro coordination, CKD/SKD assembly kit clearances, and testing equipment.

  • • Luxury and homologation vehicle import customs clearance
  • • Just-In-Time (JIT) line-side component delivery to OEM plants
  • • Enclosed vehicle transport trailers and container wheel chocking

Exhibitions, Media Equipment & ATA Carnet

Temporary import-export procedures for international trade expos, commercial film crews, broadcast cameras, and diplomatic missions without paying import duties or taxes.

  • • ATA Carnet counterfoil endorsement and security bond execution
  • • Stand-by customs assistance during venue setup and pack-down
  • • Strict re-export compliance reporting to prevent forfeiture

Heavy Industrial & Infrastructure Machinery

Complete turnkey logistics for transformers, turbines, reaction vessels, and heavy manufacturing equipment, including route surveys, port stevedoring, and foundation placement.

  • • Multi-axle hydraulic modular trailer transport
  • • Tandem mobile crane lift and marine survey certification
  • • Intermodal road, rail, and coastal barge solutions
