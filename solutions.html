Specialized Cargo & Industry Solutions

Dedicated logistics protocols engineered for high-value, sensitive, and sector-specific operational requirements across global trade corridors.

10 Core Industry Sectors

Engineered Sector Competence

Every vertical is managed with domain-specific handling, compliance certifications, and precision tracking.

Sector 01

Aviation & AOG

Time-critical aviation cargo and aircraft parts. We coordinate 24/7 emergency dispatch for Aircraft on Ground (AOG) situations, handling rotables, avionics, landing gear parts, and engine components with expedited airside customs and tarmac clearance.

Sector 02

Electronics & IT

Servers, data-center equipment, telecom and high-value electronics. Providing climate-controlled warehousing, anti-static packing, high-security sealed container transport, and shock-monitored transit for hyperscale computing assets.

Sector 03

FTWZ & SEZ

Specialized warehousing, customs and trade logistics. Duty-deferred inventory staging, import/export logistics, customs coordination, and re-export support across Free Trade Warehousing Zones and Special Economic Zones.

Sector 04

Media & Broadcast

Cameras, broadcast, media and event equipment. Coordinating ATA Carnet customs clearances, temporary importation bonds, time-critical event venue delivery, and seamless round-trip re-exportation for international broadcast teams.

Sector 05

Garments & Textiles

Import, export, warehousing and distribution. Managing apparel quality control, carton and pallet staging, container stuffing, and scheduled freight forwarding for seasonal fashion retailers and industrial fabric exporters.

Sector 06

Engineering & Industrial

Machinery, equipment, components and project cargo. Complete "Factory to Foundation" project management with multi-axle trailer haulage, route clearance surveys, and breakbulk vessel charters.

Sector 07

Automotive

Vehicles, auto components, spare parts and accessories. Synchronized Just-in-Time line-haul, customs expedited release, and secure warehousing for automotive OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers.

Sector 08

Chemicals

Specialized handling, storage and transportation for chemical cargo. Strict compliance with regulatory standards, Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS), and proper containment protocols.

Sector 09

General Cargo

End-to-end freight, customs, warehousing and transportation. Full container loads (FCL), general manufactured goods, retail inventory, and domestic road drayage.

Sector 10

Reverse Logistics

Returns, repairs, replacements and re-export coordination. Handling commercial returns, defect evaluation, warranty replacement loops, and customs re-export compliance.
