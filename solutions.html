Specialized Cargo & Industry Solutions

Dedicated logistics protocols engineered for high-value, sensitive, and sector-specific operational requirements across global trade corridors.

10 Core Industry Sectors

Engineered Sector Competence

Every vertical is managed with domain-specific handling, compliance certifications, and precision tracking.

Sector 01

Aviation & AOG Cargo

Time-critical aviation cargo, aircraft engines, and aircraft spare parts. We coordinate 24/7 emergency dispatch for Aircraft on Ground (AOG) situations, handling rotables, avionics, landing gear assemblies, and turbofan engine components on transport cradles with expedited airside customs and tarmac clearance.

Aircraft Turbofan Engines & Heavy Rotables Shock-mounted transport cradles, airside crane loading & 24/7 AOG dispatch.
Aviation Spares, Avionics & Flight Crates Temperature-sensitive avionics, landing gear parts & expedited gate passes.
Sector 02

IT & Telecom Hardware

High-density servers, data-center equipment, enterprise networking infrastructure, and telecommunications equipment. Providing climate-controlled warehousing, anti-static packing, high-security sealed container transport, and shock-monitored transit for hyperscale computing assets.

Sector 03

Electronics & Semiconductor Components

Semiconductors, printed circuit board (PCB) assemblies, precision testing instruments, and consumer electronics. Handled with electrostatic discharge (ESD) protective packaging, clean-room staging, and expedited customs clearance under statutory trade compliance.

Sector 04

Automobiles & OEM Components

Finished vehicles, powertrain assemblies, Tier-1 automotive parts, and aftermarket spares. Synchronized Just-in-Time (JIT) line-haul, customs expedited clearance, and secure warehousing for major automobile manufacturers and engineering suppliers.

Sector 05

Textiles & Garments

Import, export, protective warehousing, and distribution for the garment and textile industries. Managing apparel quality control, moisture-proof palletization, container stuffing, and scheduled international air and ocean freight forwarding.

Sector 06

Engineering & Heavy Machinery

Industrial plant equipment, precision machine tools, structural fabrications, and engineering components. Complete turnkey logistics with multi-axle trailer haulage, route clearance surveys, and specialized flat-rack/breakbulk shipping.

Sector 07

Chemicals & Hazardous Goods

Specialized handling, storage and transportation for hazardous and non-hazardous chemicals. Strict compliance with IMO, IMDG, and IATA DGR regulations, Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS), UN-certified packaging, and regulatory safety protocols.

Sector 08

Project Cargo & Over Dimensional Cargo (ODC)

Engineered turnkey movement for heavy-lift plant machinery, Over-Dimensional Cargo (ODC), out-of-gauge (OOG) consignments, and infrastructure materials. Factory-to-foundation management with route surveys, coastal barges, and heavy crane offloading.

Sector 09

FTWZ (Free Trade Warehousing Zone)

Free Trade Warehousing Zone duty-deferred inventory holding, commercial value-added activities, re-export processing, and compliant trade logistics for global suppliers and multinational importers.

Sector 10

SEZ (Special Economic Zone) Logistics

Special Economic Zone cargo handling, statutory SEZ customs clearance, Domestic Tariff Area (DTA) transfers, bond-to-bond movement, and dedicated transportation compliant with Indian SEZ Act regulations.
