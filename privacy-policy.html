Privacy Policy

How Seven Stars Logistics Private Limited protects your commercial, shipment, and personal data.

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Introduction & Commitment

Seven Stars Logistics Private Limited ("Seven Stars Logistics", "we", "our", or "us") respects the privacy and confidentiality of our clients, suppliers, consignors, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, and safeguard information submitted through our website (sevenstarslogistics.com) and our operational logistics workflows.

2. Information We Collect

We collect commercial and personal information necessary to execute international freight forwarding and customs clearance, including:

  • Contact details: Name, business email, telephone/mobile number, company name.
  • Shipment parameters: Origin, destination, cargo dimensions, weight, HS tariff codes, Incoterms, and commercial invoice information required for statutory customs filings.
  • Telemetry and server logs: IP address, browser type, and interaction timestamps.

3. Purpose of Processing

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Prepare freight quotations and verify vessel/flight scheduling.
  • Execute electronic pre-filing with Indian Customs Authorities (ICEGATE) under customs regulations.
  • Coordinate cargo handling with shipping lines, air carriers, port terminals, and Container Freight Stations (CFS).
  • Communicate operational status and tracking telemetry.

4. Data Confidentiality & Disclosure

We do not sell, lease, or monetize commercial shipping data. Information is disclosed solely to statutory regulatory authorities (Customs, port authorities), accredited shipping carriers, and verified logistics partners strictly as needed to complete the contract of carriage.

5. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational security controls to protect cargo manifests and client data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
