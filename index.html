Who We Are

Your Single-Window Import-Export Partner Since 2009

M/s Seven Stars Logistics Pvt Ltd was established in 2009 with a single-minded commitment: to set the highest standard in international freight forwarding and supply chain solutions.

Our Methodology

The 5-Step Single-Window Logistics Lifecycle

A disciplined, transparent workflow engineered to ensure strict compliance with sailing deadlines, flight schedules, and statutory customs regulations.

01

Consultation & Survey

Evaluating commodity classification, IATA DGR criteria, HS codes, and optimal inter-modal route selection.

02

Pre-Filing & Booking

Electronic pre-filing with Customs Authorities, space booking with ocean/air carriers, and documentation prep.

03

Multimodal Transit

Origin pickup, export packaging, container drayage to port/airport, and global air/ocean freight transit.

04

Port & CHA Clearance

Immediate in-house clearance directly at Chennai Customs House and port gate terminal with security sealing.

05

Site Delivery & 3PL

Bonded or general warehousing redistribution, final-mile delivery at project site, and complete closure reporting.

Proven Differentiation

Why Businesses Choose Seven Stars Logistics

Four structural advantages that make Seven Stars the logistics partner of choice for Indian and international corporate shippers.

In-House Licensed Customs House Agent (CHA)

Unlike logistics brokers who outsource customs filing to third parties, Seven Stars Logistics holds its own CHA license and is registered with CCHAA. This guarantees direct control over documentation, zero third-party markups, and strict compliance with Indian Customs rules and Allied Acts.

Port & Customs House Adjacency

Our operational headquarters is situated at 2nd Floor, Door No:2, Krishnan Koil St, George Town, directly next to Chennai Customs House and opposite the Chennai Sea Port entrance. When shipments require physical inspection or urgent endorsement, our personnel are on-site in minutes.

IATA DGR Qualified Dangerous Goods Expertise

Our customer support and operational teams are formally certified in IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR) and Customs House Agents Regulations. We handle hazardous, restricted, aircraft spares, and temperature-sensitive freight with rigorous regulatory adherence.

Pan-India Air & Sea Consolidation

Armed with consolidation licenses valid for all airports and sea ports across India, Seven Stars coordinates nationwide buyer consolidations, ICD movements, and multi-hub logistics seamlessly from our Chennai headquarters.
