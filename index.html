Free Trade Warehousing & Special Economic Zones

FTWZ & SEZ Logistics Solutions

We provide warehousing, inventory management, customs coordination, and trade logistics support for businesses utilizing Free Trade Warehousing Zones (FTWZ) and Special Economic Zones (SEZ).

FTWZ Logistics Duty-deferred warehousing, inventory holding, import/export cargo handling, and re-export support where applicable.
SEZ Logistics Special Economic Zone cargo handling, statutory documentation, dedicated transportation, and customs clearance coordination.
Regulatory Standard: We support overseas businesses with India logistics and operational requirements, subject to applicable government regulations and customer-specific compliance requirements.
