Licensed Indian Customs Brokerage at Major Airport Terminals

Direct licensed customs brokerage operations stationed at India's key Air Cargo Complexes (ACCs) manage electronic ICEGATE filings, prior Bill of Entry processing, airport examination, and expedited gate pass generation:

  • Air Waybill (AWB) Tracking: Milestone alerts from departure tarmac to destination customs release
