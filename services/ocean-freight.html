Ocean Freight Forwarding

Export & import maritime transport solutions. FCL, LCL groupage, buyer's consolidation, and break bulk handling with port-side execution at Chennai Port.

Maritime Capability

Reliable Sailing Schedules & Full Container Management

Seven Stars Logistics Pvt Ltd delivers complete ocean freight solutions connecting Indian ports — Chennai, Ennore, Kattupalli, Tuticorin, Nhava Sheva, and Visakhapatnam — with major commercial trade lanes across Europe, the Americas, the Middle East, and Asia Pacific.

Our strategic headquarters facing the Chennai Sea Port allows us to maintain close operational ties with major container lines, port terminal operators, and Container Freight Stations (CFS). We safeguard container space, expedite gate-in procedures, and coordinate physical vessel load plans with zero delay.

Meeting Sailing Deadlines: "We enable you to meet sailing deadlines that are so much a part of this trade. In fact, we go the extra mile to maintain delivery dates as per customer instructions."
Sea Freight Products

Verified Ocean Solutions

Flexible containerized and break bulk solutions tailored to your cargo profile.

FCL (Full Container Load)

Dedicated 20ft, 40ft, High Cube, Open Top, and Flat Rack containers with secured liner space allocations and preferential detention terms.

LCL & Groupage Consolidation

Cost-effective consolidation for smaller shipments that do not require an entire container, with reliable scheduled de-consolidation at destination CFS.

Buyer's Consolidation

Aggregating goods from multiple regional manufacturers into single container loads, reducing origin handling and destination customs entries.

Door-to-Door & Port-to-Port

Complete carriage encompassing factory pickup, inland drayage, port clearance, ocean voyage, and final delivery to destination receiving bays.

Break Bulk Movement

Handling out-of-gauge equipment, steel coils, machinery, and non-containerized cargo with direct hook-on-hook vessel stevedoring supervision.

Dangerous Goods Sea Transport

Strict IMDG Code compliance, dangerous goods declarations (DGD), container segregation, and maritime safety placard verification.
