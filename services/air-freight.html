Air Freight Forwarding

Export and import air freight forwarding solutions. Operating with our own Consolidation License valid across all Indian airports, supported by IATA DGR qualified specialists.

Service Overview

Speed, Regulatory Precision & Pan-India Consolidation

When delivery timelines are non-negotiable, Seven Stars Logistics Pvt Ltd provides direct, reliable air freight solutions engineered to protect your supply chain. We maintain authorized air consolidation licenses across all major international airports in India, including Chennai (MAA), Mumbai (BOM), Bangalore (BLR), and Delhi (DEL).

Whether moving urgent time-critical aerospace rotable parts, delicate electronic consignments, or dangerous goods, our qualified personnel handle flight space reservation, air waybill issuance, and direct customs airside clearance without third-party handoffs.

IATA DGR Certified Personnel: Our customer support and cargo handling teams are certified in IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR) to ensure flawless packaging, labeling, Shipper's Declaration compliance, and airline acceptance.
What We Provide

Air Freight Products & Specialized Solutions

Every product below represents our verified operational capabilities.

Door-to-Door & Airport-to-Airport

Turnkey transit from the shipper's warehouse floor to final consignee delivery, or flexible airport apron-to-apron freight forwarding tailored to your Incoterms.

Handling of Dangerous Goods (IATA DGR)

Certified packaging, UN specification labeling, documentation, and airline acceptance for hazardous, chemical, and temperature-sensitive air shipments.

Aircraft Parts & Spares (AOG)

Specialized fast-track clearance and air forwarding for aircraft parts, rotables, avionics, and mission-critical aviation components.

Exhibition, ATA Carnet & Diplomatic Cargo

Temporary import/export procedures under ATA Carnet, media equipment handling, and diplomatic pouch security protocol management.

Car Imports & Automotive Logistics

Vehicle air transport, specialized palletizing, homologation customs clearances, and luxury automotive shipment logistics.

Buyer's Air Consolidation

Consolidating multiple vendor packages into unified Master Air Waybills (MAWB), reducing customs clearance fees and airline minimum charges.
