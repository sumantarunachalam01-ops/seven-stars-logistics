In-House Customs House Brokerage (CHA)

Officially licensed Customs House Agent (CHA) and active member of CCHAA. Headquartered directly adjacent to Chennai Customs House for immediate terminal clearance.

Statutory In-House Mastery

Direct Customs Brokerage Without Third-Party Intermediaries

Customs clearance in India is governed by complex statutory frameworks, tariff schedules, notification amendments, and Allied Acts. Incomplete or incorrect documentation causes punitive port demurrage, container detention, and painful supply chain interruptions.

M/s Seven Stars Logistics Pvt Ltd operates with our own Customs House Agent (CHA) License and is registered with the Chennai Customs House Agents Association (CCHAA). Our licensed customs professionals execute import bills of entry, export shipping bills, duty assessments, and dock examinations entirely in-house.

Zero Transit Proximity Advantage: Our operational office at 2nd Floor, Door No:2, Krishnan Koil St, George Town sits immediately next to the Chennai Customs House and opposite Chennai Sea Port. Our staff can attend customs queries and terminal inspection sheds instantly.
