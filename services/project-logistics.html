Project Logistics & Over-Dimensional Cargo

Turnkey engineering, route feasibility surveys, heavy-lift transportation, and specialized customs clearance for mission-critical infrastructure projects.

Engineering & Heavy Lift

Specialized Routing & Flawless Customs Execution

Seven Stars Logistics Pvt Ltd realizes project logistics success through the powerful combination of expertise, knowledge, and experience. Our experience enables us to identify the most cost-effective route, allowing our clients to ensure the timely delivery of their equipment as well as security while in transit.

Depending on cargo and route requirements, the scope of services can be extended to encompass inter-modal solutions, as well as customs clearance and reporting capabilities. This gives us an edge, as incomplete or wrong customs documentation can cause considerable delay and cost to the delivery process, reducing supply chain reliability.

What We Provide

Core Project Cargo Solutions

Engineered transport management for heavy machinery, power generation, and industrial plants.

Route Surveys & Feasibility

Physical road surveys, bridge load assessments, overhead wire clearances, turning radius analysis, and local authority permits across state corridors.

Over-Dimensional Cargo (ODC)

Hydraulic multi-axle modular trailers, low-bed and semi-low bed carriers engineered to move out-of-gauge heavy machinery with escort safety vehicles.

Special Economic Zones (SEZ) Cargo

Specialized movements into SEZ developer zones, including zero-duty bonding, procurement documentation, and SEZ customs approvals.

Port Stevedoring & Heavy Lift Cranes

Direct ship-to-trailer tandem crane lifting, heavy tackle rigging, lashing certification, and marine surveyor approvals at Chennai Port.

Intermodal Road & Rail Coordination

Combining coastal barge, heavy rail rakes, and highway hydraulic transport to deliver heavy components to remote inland project sites.

Foundation Delivery & Jacking

Final-mile precision alignment, hydraulic jacking, sliding, and offloading onto client plant foundations.
