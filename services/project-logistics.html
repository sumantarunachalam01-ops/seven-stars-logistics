Factory to Foundation

Complete Project Solutions

Seven Stars Logistics provides a complete project solution by monitoring project activity from "Factory to Foundation". We assist clients with every phase of a project including planning, consultation, heavy lift operations, sourcing, warehousing, full and part charters, insurance, export packing, customs clearance, and documentation.

Our Global Projects cover the full range of project freight management and transportation of equipment and materials from the suppliers' point of delivery to receipt at jobsite, warehouse, or any delivery location.

Turnkey Consultation Route survey, bridge load calculations & site feasibility
Extreme Weight & Oversized Specialized multi-axle trailers and heavy-lift equipment
Operational Protocol

Factory to Foundation Execution Phases

  • 01 Engineering & Route Clearance: Bridge loading calculations, civil obstacle clearance, and turning radius engineering.
  • 02 Multi-Axle Hydraulic Haulage: Specialized modular pullers and heavy prime movers for extreme weight shipments.
  • 03 Port Stevedoring & Charters: Heavy-lift vessel loading, gearless cargo crane operations, and breakbulk charters.
  • 04 Seaworthy Packing & Lashing: Heavy structural timber framing, welding, chain lashing, and vacuum packaging.
  • 05 Foundation Setting: Precise hydraulic jacking, skidding, and setting directly onto civil foundations.
