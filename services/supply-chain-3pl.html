Single-Window Supply Chain & 3PL Integration

"Your search for SINGLE-WINDOW Import-Export solution providers ends at SSL." Integrated origin pickup, export packing, international transport, customs clearance, and site delivery.

End-to-End Integration

One Contract, One Accountability, Zero Friction

Managing international supply chains typically entails juggling multiple disconnected vendors: local truckers, packaging agents, separate customs brokers, international shipping lines, and destination warehouses. When delays occur, each party shifts blame.

Seven Stars Logistics Pvt Ltd solves this by uniting every supply chain component under one roof. Our logistics cycle covers Pickup, Packing, Transport, Customs Clearance, Site Delivery, and 3PL Warehousing, giving you a single trusted point of contact throughout the cargo journey.

The SSL Single-Window Promise: "We have been handling projects, freight forwarding, and customs clearance as total logistics as a single window service under one roof."
The 6 Pillars

What Our Single-Window Encompasses

A unified chain of custody from your manufacturing bay to final destination assembly line.

01

Factory Pick Up

Dedicated collection directly from production bays and supplier locations across pan-India industrial corridors.

02

Industrial Packing

Export seaworthy wooden crating, fumigation certification (ISPM-15), moisture-barrier vacuum packing, and lashing.

03

Multimodal Transport

Seamless intermodal drayage connecting regional factories via dedicated highway trailers, rail rakes, and port berths.

04

In-House Customs Clearance

Direct execution under our own CHA license and CCHAA membership, eliminating brokerage delays and extra markups.

05

3PL Warehousing

Bonded and general warehousing facilities near CFS terminals and inland container depots for stock buffer control.

06

Delivery at Site

Final-mile transportation with offloading, equipment placement on foundation, and clean POD documentation return.
