Comprehensive Sector Capabilities

Specialized Industry Logistics Coverage

Seven Stars Logistics handles specialized logistics and supply chain execution engineered specifically for high-compliance commercial sectors.

1. Aviation & AOG

Aircraft on Ground (AOG) emergency logistics, aircraft engines, aircraft spare parts, rotables, and priority airside customs clearance.

2. Information Technology (IT)

Hyperscale data servers, networking racks, IT hardware, secure bonded transit, and temperature-controlled handling.

3. Electronics

Semiconductors, printed circuit boards (PCBs), telecom devices, shock-monitored transit, and anti-static packaging.

4. Automobiles

OEM tier-1 automotive components, powertrains, CKD/SKD assembly kits, and just-in-time (JIT) production delivery.

5. Textiles & Garments

Apparel export logistics, carton and pallet consolidation, Garment-on-Hanger (GOH), and scheduled multi-modal sailings.

6. Engineering

Heavy machinery, precision tooling, plant infrastructure components, breakbulk chartering, and multi-axle trailers.

7. Chemicals & Dangerous Goods

IATA DGR certified cargo, UN packaging compliance, MSDS documentation, and specialized hazard-rated transport.

8. Project Cargo / ODC

Over-Dimensional Cargo (ODC), factory foundation positioning, crane rigging, barge movement, and heavy haulage.

9. FTWZ Logistics

Free Trade Warehousing Zones, duty-deferred inventory holding, value-added packaging, and international re-export.

10. SEZ Cargo Handling

Special Economic Zone cargo logistics, bonded transshipment, statutory paperwork, and direct manufacturer clearances.
