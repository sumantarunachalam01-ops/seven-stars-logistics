Packaging Engineering

Packing Categories & Scope

  • ✓ General Packing: Corrugated carton boxes, multiwall sacks, and standardized treated pallets.
  • ✓ Heavy-Duty Packing: ISPM-15 wooden crates, reinforced cases, and structural steel skids.
  • ✓ Protective Packing: Heavy-gauge bubble wrap, foam inserts, shrink wrap, and vacuum barrier sealing.
  • ✓ Container Packing: Professional stuffing, multi-point lashing, dunnage airbags, and choke blocking.
  • ✓ Specialized Packing: Fragile instruments, oversized heavy equipment, DGR hazmat, and seaworthy export packing.
