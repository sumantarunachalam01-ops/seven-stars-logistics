Why Choose Seven Stars Logistics

In international supply chains, operational integrity matters more than marketing promises. Here are the verified structural advantages that protect your cargo and sailing deadlines.

Advantage 01

In-House Customs House Agent (CHA) License

Many freight forwarders act merely as sales intermediaries, subcontracting customs clearance to unrelated third-party brokers. This disconnect causes miscommunication, unmonitored delays, and unexpected costs when customs officers raise valuation or classification queries.

Seven Stars Logistics holds its own CHA License and is an active member of CCHAA (Chennai Customs House Agents Association). Our licensed customs brokers file your documents directly on ICEGATE, liaise in person with customs appraisers, and stand with full statutory accountability for your goods.

Advantage 02

Port & Customs House Proximity: Zero Transit Delay

Our operational headquarters is located at 2nd Floor, Door No:2, Krishnan Koil St, George Town, Chennai — directly adjacent to the Chennai Customs House and facing the primary entrance of the Chennai Sea Port.

When an assessment query is raised or a container must be examined at the port CFS shed, our operational executives attend in minutes. While agents located in distant suburban offices lose full business days in transit, we resolve queries in real time.

Advantage 03

IATA DGR Certified & Regulatory Qualified Staff

Our customer support and operations teams possess formal qualifications under IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR) and Customs House Agents Regulations.

From Class 9 dangerous goods to complex Over-Dimensional Cargo (ODC) and ATA Carnet temporary imports, our team identifies compliance requirements prior to cargo handling, avoiding punitive demurrage and legal non-compliance.

Advantage 04

Pan-India Air & Sea Consolidation Authority

We possess authorized Consolidation Licenses valid for all airports and seaports across India. Operating from our central headquarters in Chennai, we handle inland cargo origins and regional manufacturing hubs seamlessly.

Whether your cargo moves through Chennai, Ennore, Kattupalli, Tuticorin, Mumbai, or Bangalore, Seven Stars coordinates complete buyer consolidation and inland drayage under unified oversight.

Advantage 05

True Single-Window Accountability

"Your search for SINGLE-WINDOW Import-Export solution providers ends at SSL." We handle Pickup, Packing, Multimodal Transport, Customs Clearance, Site Delivery, and 3PL Warehousing under one roof.

You receive a single point of operational contact, unified billing, and absolute clarity across every stage of the logistics cycle.
